Questions, answered

Good to know.

What does a real estate developer need besides a website?

A website brings enquiries in, but the sale is won in follow-up. Most developers also need a CRM for leads and site visits, live inventory, and an app for buyers or channel partners. We build them as one connected system.

Can you keep the modules of the CRM we use today?

Yes. If you share how your current CRM is organised, we match those modules first and then add the real estate and AI features on top, so your team does not have to relearn everything.

Do you build for developers in the UAE?

Yes. We build for developers in India and the UAE and quote in AED for UAE clients. The work is delivered remotely with regular demos.

Is there an app for channel partners and brokers?

Yes. Partners can register a buyer, which records the time of the claim, follow the status and see their brokerage. This reduces disputes over who brought the buyer.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many projects, apps and modules you need. Every build is quoted after a free consultation with a written scope and a fixed price.

Can I see it before deciding?

Yes. The real estate demo on this site is open to everyone. Click through the website, the apps and the CRM, then tell us what you would change for your business.
