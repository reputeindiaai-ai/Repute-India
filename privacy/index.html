Privacy policy

This page explains what information Repute India AI collects on this website, why, and the choices you have. Last updated: 17 November 2026.

Who we are

Repute India AI, 401, Besides Chamunda Restaurant, Aman Square, Petrol Pump, behind Fatehgunj, Fatehgunj, Vadodara, Gujarat 390008, India. Email support@reputeindiaai.com, phone and WhatsApp +91 97371 33331.

What we collect

  • Enquiries. When you use the enquiry form we receive what you type: your name, phone number, email, business name, the service and budget you choose, and your message. The form saves these details and opens WhatsApp so you can send them to us.
  • Messages. If you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email, we keep that conversation so we can reply and follow up.
  • Accounts. If you register for a client account, we store the details you enter: your name, business name, business category, phone number, email and city.
  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site: pages viewed, the type of device and browser, and an approximate location. It uses cookies. For visitors in the UK and the EU, analytics stays off unless you press Accept.

What we use it for

To reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote, deliver the work you ask for, and improve this website. We do not sell your information, and we do not use advertising cookies.

Who helps us run the site

We use a small number of service providers, who process information on our behalf: Google (Analytics), Supabase (the database that stores enquiries and accounts, hosted in Singapore), Meta (WhatsApp), Resend (login emails), Render (application hosting) and GitHub (website hosting). Because of this, your information may be processed outside your own country.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and account details for as long as we need them to reply to you and to do business with you. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Email support@reputeindiaai.com to access, correct or delete your data, or to withdraw your consent. Please write from the email address or phone number you used with us, so we can find your details. You can also write to us at the address above or message +91 97371 33331.
