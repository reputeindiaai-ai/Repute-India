Questions, answered

Good to know.

Does a small business really need an app?

Not always. An app makes sense when customers come back often, such as for bookings, orders, memberships or loyalty, or when staff work away from a desk. We will tell you if a mobile-friendly website would do the job.

Do you build for both Android and iOS?

Yes. We build for both and publish to Google Play and the App Store.

What is the difference between a customer app and a staff app?

A customer app is for your buyers: booking, ordering, paying and loyalty. A staff app is for your team: tasks, attendance, visits and collections. Many businesses start with one and add the other later.

Will the app connect to our existing software?

Usually, if your current software allows it. If you do not have a system yet, we build the CRM and the app together so they share one database.

How much does an app cost?

It depends on the number of screens and features, so every app is quoted after a free consultation. You get a written scope and a fixed price in INR, AED or USD.

Can I try an example first?

Yes. The app demos on this site are open to everyone and run in your browser.
