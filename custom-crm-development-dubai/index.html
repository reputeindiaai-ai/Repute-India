Questions, answered

Good to know.

Why build a custom CRM instead of buying one?

A ready-made CRM is quicker to start, but you adapt to it and usually pay per user every month. A custom CRM matches your process exactly and contains only what your team uses. We will tell you honestly if an off-the-shelf tool is the better fit.

Do you work with businesses in Dubai remotely?

Yes. Our headquarters is in Vadodara, India, and we work with UAE businesses over video calls and WhatsApp. You get a written scope, regular demos and a named contact.

How much does custom CRM development cost in Dubai?

It depends on the number of modules and integrations, so every build is quoted after a free consultation. You receive a fixed price in AED before any work starts.

Can the CRM connect to WhatsApp?

Yes. We connect it to the official WhatsApp Business Platform so replies, reminders and follow-ups can be sent from the CRM and saved against each lead.

Can you move our data from Excel or another CRM?

Yes. Importing your existing leads, customers and history is part of the launch step, and we check the imported data with you before going live.

What happens after launch?

We stay on for support, hosting and changes under a monthly plan. As your process changes, the CRM changes with it.
