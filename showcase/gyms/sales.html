Sales script · Gyms & Fitness

Sell this in 5 minutes.

Walk a gym owner from "interested" to "let's do it." Gym owners don't have a lead problem — they have a retention problem. Sell that.

1 · The problem they feel

"I sign up 30 members in January. By April, half have quietly stopped coming — and at renewal they're gone. I'm always selling new memberships just to stand still."

Silent quitting

Members don't cancel — they just stop coming, then don't renew. The owner finds out too late.

Renewals left to chance

No system flags who's slipping before their plan ends — so winnable renewals are lost.

Upsells by luck

PT and nutrition are the real margin, but nobody knows the right member or moment.

2 · What we build for them

A retention engine — website, Churn Radar CRM, member app, and the AI Edge.

A premium dark website that converts walk-bys into free-trial sign-ups
The CRM: collections & dues up top, plus Churn Radar, renewals, trials, trainer P&L
A member app they open daily: workouts, AI diet tracker, progress, loyalty
The AI Edge: Pre-Quit · PT-Upsell Timing · Dead-Slot Filler · Nutrition Signal · Consistency-to-Renewal
3 · The ROI story

Make the money obvious — it's all retention.

0
fewer cancellations — members caught before they quit
0
more renewals saved with the runway + loyalty engine
0
more PT & nutrition upsells, perfectly timed
"Boss, keeping a member is 5× cheaper than finding a new one. If this saves even 15 members from quitting and 10 renewals a month, that's lakhs a year — and it runs while you're on the floor coaching."
4 · Why we're different
The one-liner that lands

"Every gym software tells you who paid. Ours tells you who's about to leave — and hands you the message to bring them back, before it costs you the renewal."

And the proof is real: we built and run the Chahal Academy operating system — a full management platform live in production. We don't just demo software; we run it.

5 · How to present (in order)

Run the demo like this.

1

Lead with the Churn Radar

Don't open with the website. Open the CRM. "This is every member about to quit your gym — with a win-back already written." Let that silence sit.

2

Show collections & dues

"And it still does the basics beautifully — today's collection, who owes you, attendance." Owners need to trust it runs the gym, not just predicts.

3

Open the member app

"This is why they keep coming — workouts, an AI diet tracker, a loyalty reward for consistency. A member who opens the app doesn't quit."

4

Land on the AI Edge

Walk the five AI features slowly — Pre-Quit, PT-Upsell, Dead-Slot, Nutrition, Consistency-to-Renewal. "No other gym software does this."

★

Close with the question

"If this saved even 15 members a month from quitting — what's that worth to you over a year?" Then stop talking.
