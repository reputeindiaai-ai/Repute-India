Sales script · Restaurants & Cafes

Sell this in 5 minutes.

Walk a restaurant owner from "interested" to "let's do it." Restaurant owners don't have a food problem — they have a repeat-guest and a commission problem. Sell that.

1 · The problem they feel

"I serve 200 people on a Saturday and have no idea who any of them are. Zomato takes a quarter of every delivery. One angry review tanks my rating. And weekends are chaos at the door. I'm full — but I'm not building anything."

Guests vanish forever

Hundreds dine, pay, and leave as strangers. No database, no way to bring them back — every night starts from zero.

Zomato eats the margin

~25% commission on every delivery, and the customer belongs to the app, not the restaurant.

Reviews & rush hurt

One bad public review sinks the rating, and the weekend door is a clipboard scramble that loses walk-ins.

2 · What we build for them

A guest-keeping engine — website, Rush Manager CRM, guest app, and the AI Edge.

A warm, appetising website that turns searches into table bookings and direct orders
The CRM: reservations, collections & billing up top, plus the Smart Waitlist, win-back, review rescue & direct ordering
A guest app they keep: table booking, digital menu, photo rewards & loyalty
The AI Edge: Smart Waitlist · Photo Reward Engine · Review Rescue · Guest Win-Back · Direct Ordering · Empty-Slot Filler

Make the money obvious — it's repeat guests + saved commission.

4 · Why we're different
The one-liner that lands

"Every restaurant system tells you what you sold. Ours tells you who you served — and brings them back, pulls them off Zomato, and stops a bad review before it ever goes public."

5 · How to present (in order)

Run the demo like this.

1

Open the website on their phone

Food sells on appetite. Pull up the warm, photo-led site with live table booking. "This is what a guest sees before they choose you — and it books them in one tap."

2

Lead with the Rush Manager

"It's Saturday, 11 parties waiting, no clipboard. Guests check themselves in by QR, and the system WhatsApps them the moment their table's ready." Then point to the walk-out warning. Let it sink in.

3

Show the basics still shine

"And it runs the floor beautifully — tonight's bookings, today's collection, every open bill, your whole guest database." Owners need to trust it runs the restaurant, not just predicts.

4

Land on the AI Edge

Walk them slowly: the Photo Reward turning guests into marketers, Review Rescue catching the 2★ before Google sees it, the Sharma-family win-back, and the ₹1.5L of Zomato commission saved. "No other restaurant software does this."

"If this turned even 40 of this weekend's strangers into regulars, and saved you a lakh a month off Zomato — what's that worth over a year?" Then stop talking.
