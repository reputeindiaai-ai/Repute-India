No-Show Shield

End the empty-chair problem.

Smart WhatsApp reminders go out a day before and a few hours before, each with a one-tap reschedule link so patients rebook instead of ghosting. The AI flags who's most likely to skip, so your desk confirms them personally.

◆
Example: "Mr. Patel has missed his last 2 appointments and hasn't confirmed today's. High no-show risk — active confirmation call drafted before his slot goes empty."
🔁
Recall / Follow-up Engine

Bring patients back on time.

The system watches who's due — six-month check-ups, chronic reviews, vaccinations — surfaces the revenue it represents, and drafts the recall message ready to send. Returning patients are the cheapest revenue a clinic has.

◆
Example: "23 patients are due for follow-up this month = ₹2.3 lakh in recall revenue. Personalised messages drafted — one tap to send each."
⭐
Real-time Review Engine

Protect your reputation, live.

After each visit a quick rating goes out. Happy patients (4–5★) are guided to post on Google; unhappy ones (1–3★) come privately to the doctor first — so a bad experience is fixed, not published.

◆
Example: "A 2★ for a long wait was caught privately before it went public — a recovery message from Dr. Mehta is drafted, and a happy patient was nudged to post instead."

Waiting-Room Queue

A calm waiting room, on their phone.

Patients see their live queue position and estimated time from their own phone — so they can wait in the car or nearby instead of crowding your reception. Calmer clinic, better experience, better reviews.

◆
Example: "You're 2nd in the queue, ~10 minutes away — we'll alert you before you're up. No crowded waiting room."
