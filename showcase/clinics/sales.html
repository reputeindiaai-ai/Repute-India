Sales script · Clinics & Doctors

Sell this in 5 minutes.

Walk a doctor from "interested" to "let's do it." Doctors don't have a patient-shortage problem — they have a leaky calendar. Sell that.

1 · The problem they feel

"My chairs sit empty when patients don't show. Half of them never come back for their follow-up. And my front desk is on the phone all day instead of looking after the people in the room. I'm busy, but the calendar still leaks money."

The leak is a connected cycle — and it compounds:

Leak 1

No-shows

Patients book, then forget or ghost. Empty chairs that can't be refilled at short notice.

→ No-Show Shield reminds & rebooks them
Leak 2

No return

Patients never come back for follow-ups, reviews or check-ups — the cheapest revenue, just walking away.

→ Recall Engine brings them back on time
Leak 3

Weak reputation

Few reviews and the odd bad one mean fewer new patients find and trust the clinic.

→ Review Engine builds & protects it

Each leak feeds the next: empty chairs, fewer returns, a weaker reputation, fewer new patients. We break the cycle at every point.

5 · How to present (in order)

Run the demo like this.

1

Name the three leaks first

Before showing anything, say it plainly: "Your calendar leaks money three ways — no-shows, patients who never return, and a reputation that doesn't pull new ones in." Watch them nod. Now they're listening.

2

Lead with the No-Show Shield

Open the CRM. "This is every appointment at risk today, reminded automatically, with a reschedule link so they rebook instead of vanishing — and the AI flags Mr. Patel, who's missed twice, to confirm personally." Let it land.

3

Show the basics still shine

"And it runs the clinic beautifully — today's appointments, collections, billing, records and e-prescriptions." Doctors must trust it runs the practice, not just predicts.

4

Open the Recall Engine, then the app

"23 patients are due back this month — that's ₹2.3 lakh, with the messages already written." Then the patient app: live queue, prescriptions, recall nudge. "This is why they come back."

★

Close with the question

"If this saved even 5 no-shows a week and brought back 20 patients a month — what's that worth to you over a year?" Then stop talking.
