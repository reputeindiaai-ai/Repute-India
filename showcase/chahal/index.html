Before

A growing academy outgrowing its tools.

Enquiries scattered across phones and notebooks. Admissions tracked in spreadsheets. Fees, attendance and faculty schedules in separate places. As the academy scaled, the cracks widened — and good leads slipped through them.

— Enquiries lost between counsellors
— Admission status nobody could see at a glance
— Fee dues chased manually, every month
— Attendance and batches on paper
— No single source of truth for management
The system we built

One platform, seven jobs.

Every part of running the academy, connected — so a single enquiry flows cleanly into a tracked admission, an enrolled student, and a managed account.

🎯

Lead Management

Every enquiry captured, assigned to a counsellor, and followed up — with status visible end to end. No lead forgotten.

📝

Admissions

From application to confirmation: forms, documents, course selection and approvals tracked in one pipeline.

🎓

Student Management

A complete profile per student — batch, course, documents, history and communication in one record.

💰

Fee Tracking

Fee plans, instalments, dues and receipts — with automatic reminders so collections stay on time.

🗓️

Attendance

Batch-wise attendance captured and reported — visibility for faculty, management and parents.

👩‍🏫

Faculty Coordination

Schedules, batch allocation and lecture coordination — the teaching team aligned and on time.

📊

Reporting

Live dashboards on admissions, revenue, attendance and pipeline — management decisions on real numbers.

✦

All connected

Not seven tools — one system where every module talks to the others.
