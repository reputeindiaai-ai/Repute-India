Questions, answered

Good to know.

What does Repute India AI build?

We design and build websites, CRMs, custom business systems, mobile apps, AI agents, WhatsApp automation, reputation management, business process automation and e-commerce stores. We also run Get Found in AI Search, a monthly service that gets your business named when customers ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google for a recommendation.

What is Get Found in AI Search?

When a buyer asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google for the best service in their city, the AI names the businesses it keeps seeing across trusted websites. Get Found in AI Search makes sure your business is one of those names, and that you also show up on Google. Plans start at ₹4,999 a month.

Can you guarantee my business will be number one in ChatGPT?

No, and nobody honestly can. AI answers change daily, and anyone guaranteeing a rank is guessing. What we promise is every deliverable in your plan, on time, and an honest monthly report with real screenshots of where you appear.

How long does it take to show up in AI answers?

AI answers usually take 3 to 6 months to change. That is why the minimum term is 6 months: a shorter contract would end before you see results.

Which countries do you work in?

We serve businesses across India, the UAE, East Africa, the UK and worldwide. Everything is delivered remotely, and Get Found in AI Search has local-currency pricing for India, the UAE, the UK, Australia and the USA.
