Questions, answered

Good to know.

What is GEO, or generative engine optimisation?

GEO is the work of getting a business mentioned in answers from AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. It builds on SEO, and adds consistent profiles, expert content and mentions on the websites those tools rely on.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims at a position in a list of links. GEO aims at being named inside the answer itself. Much of the groundwork is shared, so we work on both, including your Google presence.

Can you guarantee my business will be named?

No. AI answers change daily, and anyone guaranteeing a position is guessing. We promise every deliverable in your plan, on time, and an honest monthly report with real screenshots.

How long does it take to see results?

AI answers usually take 3 to 6 months to change. That is why the minimum term is 6 months: a shorter contract would end before you see results.

How much does it cost?

Plans start at ₹4,999 a month in India, AED 799 in the UAE and £179 in the UK, plus a one-time setup fee. Sponsored and media fees are extra, billed at cost and always approved by you first.

Do you use fake reviews or paid rankings?

Never. We do not use fake reviews, made-up awards or paid lists presented as independent rankings. Paid placements are always labelled.
