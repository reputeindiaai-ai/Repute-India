Questions, answered

Good to know.

What is a custom CRM?

It is customer-management software built for one business instead of bought as a standard product. It follows your process, uses your terms and includes only the modules you need.

Is a custom CRM worth it for a small business?

It is when a spreadsheet no longer copes and a standard CRM does not fit how you sell. If a ready-made tool would serve you well, we will say so in the consultation.

How long does it take to build?

It depends on the number of modules. You receive a written scope with a timeline and a fixed price before work starts, and you see working screens along the way.

Can it work on mobile?

Yes. The CRM works in a phone browser, and we can also build a dedicated mobile app for staff or customers.

Can it send WhatsApp messages?

Yes. We connect the CRM to the official WhatsApp Business Platform for replies, reminders and follow-ups, saved against each customer.

Which countries do you serve?

We serve businesses across India, the UAE, East Africa, the UK and worldwide. Work is delivered remotely from our headquarters in Vadodara, India.
