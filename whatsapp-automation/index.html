Questions, answered

Good to know.

What is WhatsApp automation?

It means WhatsApp messages are sent or answered by software rather than typed by a person each time. Common uses are instant replies, appointment and payment reminders, follow-ups and review requests.

Is it allowed by WhatsApp?

Yes, when it is done on the official WhatsApp Business Platform from Meta, which is what we use. We do not use unofficial tools that send bulk messages from a personal number.

Are there message charges from Meta?

Meta charges for certain business-initiated messages, and the rates depend on the country and message type. We explain these costs in your quote before you commit.

Can we still reply to customers ourselves?

Yes. Your team can take over any conversation at any time. Automation handles the routine messages and hands over when a person is needed.

Can it send bulk promotional messages?

It can send broadcasts to customers who have opted in, using message templates approved by Meta. We do not send messages to purchased lists or to people who have not agreed to hear from you.

Do you support payment links such as M-Pesa?

Payment links can be included in messages. For East Africa, M-Pesa payment links are available on request.
