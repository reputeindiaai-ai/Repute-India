Questions, answered

Good to know.

What is a WhatsApp CRM for a clinic?

It is a patient-management system where WhatsApp is the main channel. Enquiries, bookings, reminders and follow-ups all run through your clinic's WhatsApp number, and every conversation is saved against the patient record.

Does it work with our existing clinic number?

Yes, in most cases. We connect your number to the official WhatsApp Business Platform from Meta. We check your number during the consultation and explain what changes for your staff.

Can patients book appointments on WhatsApp?

Yes. Patients can pick a doctor and a time in the chat, and the booking appears in the CRM. Reminders are sent automatically before the visit.

Do you support M-Pesa for clinics in Kenya?

M-Pesa payment links are available on request for clinics in Kenya. Tell us how you collect payments today and we will include it in the scope.

How much does a clinic CRM cost?

Each build is quoted after a free consultation, because clinics differ in size and workflow. Quotes are in INR for India, AED for the UAE and USD elsewhere, with a written scope and a fixed price.

Which countries do you work in?

We build for clinics in India, the UAE and Kenya, and serve businesses worldwide. Everything is delivered remotely from our headquarters in Vadodara, India.
